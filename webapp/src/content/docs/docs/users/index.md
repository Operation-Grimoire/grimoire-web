Grimoire is an open-source Android novel reader. It reads web novels and EPUBs,
and expands through community-maintained **extensions** — content sources shipped
as separate apps and discovered at runtime, so the catalogue grows independently
of app updates.

## Get started

1. [Install the app](/docs/users/install).
2. [Add a repository](/docs/users/add-repositories) to get content sources.
3. Browse, fetch, and read.

## What you can do

- Organize a **library** with categories, PIN-locking, and background refresh.
- Tune the **reader** — typography, themes, orientation, and text-to-speech.
- Read **offline** with per-chapter downloads.
- Sync metadata from **NovelUpdates** and back up or migrate your library.
