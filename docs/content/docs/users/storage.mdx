---
title: Managing storage
description: Inspect app storage and clear replaceable cached data.
---

Open **More → Settings → Data management** to inspect Grimoire's storage use. Back up important library data and keep original EPUBs before large cleanup operations.

## Usage breakdown [#usage-breakdown]

The storage screen separates library and browse data, downloaded chapter text and images, cover cache, database, and leftover installer files. Use Refresh to update the displayed usage after a change. Device free space is broader than Grimoire's own usage; files held elsewhere on the phone may not be included in an app category.

To reclaim downloaded chapter space, use [Downloads](/docs/users/downloads/#retrying-and-removing) and inspect which books you still need offline. Removing an original EPUB outside Grimoire and deleting an app download are different actions.

## Clearing caches [#clearing-cache]

In **Data management**, choose the clear action for the specific category and read its confirmation:

- **Cover cache** removes cached images. Covers need downloading again when displayed.
- **Browse data** removes cached novels that are neither saved in Library nor read. Read history and downloads are kept.
- **Installer files** removes leftover app and extension APKs. Do not clear these while an installation is in progress.

These actions are different from Android's **Clear storage** action, which removes app data. A cache cleanup is also different from deleting downloaded chapters: deleting chapter downloads removes their offline copies.

If space remains low, inspect completed downloads and files outside the app rather than repeatedly clearing the same cache. Covers and source content can use space again when fetched. Check that the books you need still open offline after deleting their local content.
