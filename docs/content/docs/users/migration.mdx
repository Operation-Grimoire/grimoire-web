title: Moving between sources

Migration replaces a source novel in your library with a matching novel from another source. [Make a backup](/docs/users/backups/) before migrating a book with important progress.

## Moving between sources [#moving-between-sources]

1. Install the destination source and check that its novel and chapters load.
2. Open the saved novel's detail menu and choose the migration action.
3. Search for the same novel and open the destination result.
4. Choose **Migrate here** and review the confirmation, including chapter matches.
5. Confirm only after checking the title, source, and match result.
6. Open the migrated novel and inspect its chapters and reading position.

Matched chapters can carry read progress. Progress for chapters that do not match cannot transfer. A confirmation with no matches means no chapter progress will carry over. Different numbering, split chapters, or different editions can make a superficially similar novel a poor migration target.

The confirmation identifies the old entry that will leave your library. Cancel if it is the wrong book or the match is unsafe. Migration does not copy source credentials or grant access to locked chapters on the destination site. Downloaded files from the old source are not a substitute for checking the new source's content.

If the old extension is unavailable, saved details remain useful for finding the replacement. Avoid deleting the library entry before starting migration. Keep your backup until you have checked the result; restoring a backup merges data rather than providing a one-button migration undo.
