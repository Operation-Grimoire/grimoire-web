---
title: History & statistics
description: Reopen recent books, inspect reading activity, and clear history.
---

Open **More → History** for recent reading and browsing, or **More → Statistics** for your reading activity.

## Reading history [#reading-history]

In History, choose **Reading**. Chapters you open in the reader appear here, unless incognito is active. Select an entry to return to reading. Progress and read flags belong to chapters; removing a history entry does not remove its novel or downloaded chapters.

## Browsing history [#browsing-history]

Choose **Browsing** in History. This records novels opened in Browse that you have not saved to the library. Select a novel to reopen its details. This list is separate from reading history and from your browser's website history.

## Reading statistics [#reading-statistics]

Open **More → Statistics** for chapters and words read, novels started or completed, average words, and the last-read date. The library summary shows saved novels, chapters, unread chapters, and downloaded chapters. These figures come from local records; a book's chapter percentage is not a measure of time spent reading. Marking chapters manually or restoring progress can change the underlying totals. This screen is not a cross-device activity service.

## Clearing history [#clearing-history]

Long-press a history entry to select it, then choose **Delete**. Select more entries first if needed. To clear a whole list, choose **Clear reading history** or **Clear browsing history** from the History menu and confirm. Clearing is permanent for that list; the library and chapters are kept. Clearing reading history does not also clear browsing history.

To pause future recording, use [Incognito](/docs/users/privacy/#incognito). It does not erase earlier records or prevent progress from saving.
