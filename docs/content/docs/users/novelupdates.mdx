---
title: Using NovelUpdates
description: Discover series, save listings, and find books in your installed sources.
---

NovelUpdates provides discovery information and series listings. A listing is not a book installed in your library. Grimoire is not affiliated with NovelUpdates.

## Rankings [#rankings]

Open the **NovelUpdates** rankings shortcut in Browse. Choose popular rankings for the month or all time, or activity rankings for the week, month, or all time. Use available genre and language filters to narrow the list. Open a result to inspect the series.

If shortcuts are hidden, enable **NovelUpdates shortcuts** in **Settings → Browse**. These lists require a connection to NovelUpdates.

## Latest releases [#latest-releases]

Open the NovelUpdates latest shortcut in Browse to inspect recent releases. Select a series to see its details and release information. A release listed there does not guarantee an installed extension can read it. Check the corresponding source before adding a library copy.

## Advanced search [#advanced-search]

Open NovelUpdates search from Browse, enter a title, and use **Filters** to narrow by genre, language, novel type, story status, or tags. Select **Match ALL** or **Match ANY** for tags as appropriate, then choose the sort field and direction. Clear restrictive filters if a known series is missing. Filter data and results come from NovelUpdates.

## Series and reviews [#series-and-reviews]

Open a series result for its description, alternative titles, details, releases, and reviews. Use the offered view-more actions to open additional information on NovelUpdates. Community ratings and reviews are distinct from your [personal rating](/docs/users/novel-details/#personal-rating) in Grimoire.

## Saved series [#saved-series]

Tap **Save** on a series to keep its listing in **Saved on NovelUpdates**. Use that list to return later; use its removal action to remove a saved listing. Saving a series here does not add a source novel to Library, download chapters, or synchronize a NovelUpdates website account.

## Finding source matches [#finding-source-matches]

From a series page, choose **Find in my sources**. Review the returned matches and open the correct source novel. Compare title, author, and chapter list before saving it to Library. Install a suggested extension if needed. Alternate titles and differences between catalogs can prevent a match; try [source search](/docs/users/finding-novels/) manually.

## Novel panel [#novel-panel]

Enable **Show NovelUpdates section** in **Settings → NovelUpdates** to add the panel to novel details. It can show matched listing information, such as alternative titles, rating, and recommendations. Automatic matching can be wrong. Check the linked series before relying on its details; use manual NovelUpdates search when the panel cannot find the right listing.
