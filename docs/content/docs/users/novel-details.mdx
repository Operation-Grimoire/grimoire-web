---
title: Novel details
description: Set personal ratings, edit metadata, replace covers, and share books.
---

Open a novel from Library or Browse to see its details and chapter list. Save it to your library before relying on personal edits as part of your collection.

## Personal rating [#personal-rating]

Use the personal-rating control on the novel detail screen to set your score out of ten. Change it through the same control. This is your local score, separate from the rating reported by a source or NovelUpdates. You can display it on library covers and sort or filter by it in Library.

## Editing details [#editing-details]

Long-press an editable field on the detail screen to open its editor. Title, author, description, status, and genres have personal overrides. Save your change and check the updated detail screen. Source refreshes preserve these overrides. Use **Reset to source** in the field editor to remove an override and return to the source's value.

An edited title does not change the source website or the novel's identity there. If a new chapter list is wrong, changing the title is not a substitute for fixing the source or [migrating](/docs/users/migration/).

## Custom covers [#custom-covers]

Open the cover's options and choose **Replace with image** to select a local image, or **Replace with URL** to supply an image address. Use **Reset to source cover** to remove the override. Keep the original image if you want to reuse it on another device: local cover files do not travel inside a library backup. A remote image still needs to be reachable when fetched.

## Sharing novels [#sharing-novels]

Use the detail screen's share action to prepare a novel share card, then choose a destination in Android's share sheet. Review the generated card before sending it. A card or source link helps someone find the novel; it does not transfer your library entry, account access, EPUB file, or downloaded chapters.

If displayed data is wrong, refresh the novel and check whether you have a personal override. For a source problem, report the affected URL and source version without account credentials.
